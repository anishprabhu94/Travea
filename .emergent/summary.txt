The Trāvea app is a luxury AI travel planner with Apple-glass/VisionOS aesthetics. The  (bookings.tsx) supports dynamic city management and trip statuses (Planning, Upcoming, Ongoing, Completed), with recent UI updates to quick status, city/date pills, and progress bars. The  and  pages are a seamless extension, requiring a sculpted frosted glass hero pane, cinematic stay cards, and a refined Mark as Booked/Mark as Canceled footer dock. The system must handle single/multi-stay modes, dynamic pricing, state persistence, and booking/cancellation indicators.

Key functional requirements include:
- **One Stay mode:** Cards show entire city date range; prices/availability for all nights. Booking one place covers entire city. Blocked if partial bookings exist.
- **Select Dates mode:** Pick a shorter range; cards update to selected dates. Prices/availability for selected nights only. Booked nights not selectable.
- **Availability:** A stay is bookable only if available for *every* night in range. Otherwise, Unavailable for these dates.
- **Saved (favorites):** Not a filter category. Saved cards appear in horizontal carousels at the top of their respective filter sections (or a single carousel in For You). Dates update dynamically.
- **Booking/Canceling:** Updates stay status, releases/locks dates.
- **Dynamic Updates:** Prices and availability update on mode switch, date changes, filters, traveler count, booking/cancellation.
- **UI:** Frosted glass, precise spacing, specific fonts, colors, and shadows. A floating filter cluster button replaces the old filter pane.
